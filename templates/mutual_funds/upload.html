Upload Mutual Fund Statements

Upload CAMS Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) PDF files or Three Sixty Degree Investmart / Children Transaction Checklist Excel (.xlsx, .xls) files. The ingestion service will automatically parse transactions, register new folios, and recalculate holdings.

Or search and import files directly from your Outlook application

Information & Guidelines

  • Ensure you upload the official CAS statement generated from camsonline.com or kfintech.com.
  • Supported formats: Detailed Account Statements containing transactions. Summary-only statements will update holdings but won't populate historical transaction logs.
  • The system will read the PAN inside the statement and automatically associate all folios to the matching registered person (e.g. PATRIARCH, MR, VG, APEX).
  • Automatic deduplication prevents duplicate transaction logs. You can upload the same file (or overlapping statements) without doubling your figures.
  • Once uploaded, the database executes FIFO allocations to update holding balances and cost averages immediately.
